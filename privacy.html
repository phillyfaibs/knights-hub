Knights Hub privacy

Knights Hub is a private team page for the CVHA AtoMc Golden Knights U10/U11 house league team, run by the coaching staff.

What we use

When you sign in with Google, Knights Hub receives your name and email address. We use your email only to check that you are on the team's list and to show the right view (coach or parent).

What we keep

The team's schedule, awards and game stats are kept in a private Google Sheet owned by the head coach. To help the coaches see whether the hub is useful, we keep a simple log of visits (who, when, which pages, roughly how long) and a log of edits made by coaches.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking across other sites, and nothing is sold or shared outside the coaching staff. Players are shown to parents by jersey number and first name only.

Questions or removal

Ask Coach Phil (philfaibish@gmail.com) and we will remove you from the list and your visit history.
